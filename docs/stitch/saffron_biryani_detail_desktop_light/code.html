Saffron Biryani

Our signature Saffron Biryani is a testament to the grand culinary traditions of the Subcontinent. Each serving is slow-cooked using the traditional 'Dum' method, where a sealed vessel allows the flavors to meld into a sublime harmony. We use aged extra-long grain Basmati rice, hand-selected for its fragrance and delicate texture.

The secret lies in our proprietary spice blend, featuring whole cloves, cardamom, and cinnamon, elevated by the world’s finest Kashmiri saffron. This premium variety features tender, marinated protein pieces that have been braised for hours, ensuring every bite is rich with authentic Pakistani heritage and high-end artisanal quality.
